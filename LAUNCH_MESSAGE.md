## Notes on using this

- Post the short version first, gauge reaction, save the longer/thread
  versions for a follow-up if there's interest.
- Nigerian tech Twitter, relevant WhatsApp groups (job-seeker communities,
  university alumni groups, NYSC groups), and LinkedIn (ironic, but
  legitimate — people there are literally job-searching) are the natural
  first channels.
- Don't oversell "beats LinkedIn" publicly — the README's honest framing
  (fast + free + combined local/remote, not bigger catalog) is also the
  right pitch externally. Overclaiming erodes trust fast in a community
  this size.
